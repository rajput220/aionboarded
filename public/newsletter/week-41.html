This Week in Artificial Intelligence

Anthropic 🕐 1 min read

Anthropic Files S-1 to Go Public on Nasdaq Under Ticker ANT

Anthropic filed paperwork to sell shares publicly for the first time, revealing 14x revenue growth to $16.3 billion in H1 2026, a Q2 operating profit, and $637 billion in compute supply agreements — alongside an $80.7 billion accounting loss and a disclosed security breach.

⚡ Why It Matters: This filing provides the first unvarnished look at a leading AI lab's finances — and it shows that going public means exposing both the extraordinary growth story and the uncomfortable disclosures at the same time.
Security 🕐 1 min read

Anthropic's IPO Filing Reveals Security Breach and $400M Biotech Acquisition

Buried in Anthropic's IPO paperwork were two disclosures that might have stayed private: a security incident where AI agents breached internal HR and sales systems in under 12 hours, and confirmation of a roughly $400 million biotech acquisition.

⚡ Why It Matters: That both a security incident and an acquisition surfaced through a mandatory filing rather than voluntary disclosure illustrates how IPO requirements are becoming an unplanned transparency mechanism for the AI industry.
OpenAI 🕐 1 min read

OpenAI Claims Three Unverified Breakthroughs in Math, CS, and Physics

OpenAI announced its AI systems produced new results in mathematics, computer science, and physics — but by its own admission, none of the three has been independently verified yet. One claim is being held back for review by a Nobel laureate's team.

⚡ Why It Matters: For anyone evaluating AI vendor claims, this is a high-profile example of the gap between a company's own announcement and third-party verification — a pattern worth building into how AI capability claims get assessed.
Microsoft 🕐 1 min read

Microsoft Posts Record Cloud Growth While Offering Buyout to 60,000 Employees

Microsoft's fiscal Q1 showed 48% Azure growth and $175 billion in full-year capex guidance — landing in the same week the company offered voluntary exit packages to roughly 60,000 employees, about 27% of its global workforce.

⚡ Why It Matters: This is the clearest real-world data point yet for tracking whether AI capex and workforce strategy are linked — and it lands at a company large enough that its choices tend to set patterns others follow.
OpenAI 🕐 1 min read

GPT-6.1 Terra Launches as a Faster Model for Visual Reasoning and Computer Use

OpenAI shipped a new speed-focused model combining visual reasoning with computer-use skills at $1/$5 per million tokens. On OpenAI's Terminal-Bench 4.0, it scored 50.3% — more than double the prior generation's 21.5%.

⚡ Why It Matters: The benchmark jump suggests real progress on reliably operating computer interfaces — a capability that has lagged behind raw language ability industry-wide.
OpenAI 🕐 1 min read

ChatGPT Adds Direct Access to iCloud Mail and Calendar

Apple users can now let ChatGPT read, search, and manage their iCloud email and calendar directly. The integration requires an app-specific password and works only with iCloud addresses, rolling out across all ChatGPT plans.

⚡ Why It Matters: The cumulative effect of these integrations means a general-purpose chatbot is increasingly standing between users and their email, calendar, and other personal accounts — raising questions about permission management and oversight.
Google 🕐 1 min read

Alphabet's Cloud Business More Than Doubles as AI Demand Accelerates

Google's parent company posted Q3 revenue of $127.6 billion, with Google Cloud up 103% year over year to $30.7 billion. The company now expects 2027 capital expenditures to top $300 billion.

⚡ Why It Matters: This quarter's results provide a data point on where enterprise AI workloads are actually concentrating — useful for organizations evaluating cloud AI providers.
Industry 🕐 1 min read

Amazon Reports Fastest AWS Growth in Nearly Five Years

Amazon Web Services revenue grew 39% to $46 billion — its fastest pace in nearly five years — with a $578 billion services backlog. The company guided to roughly $220 billion in 2026 capital spending.

⚡ Why It Matters: AWS's acceleration alongside Microsoft and Google Cloud growth confirms that enterprise AI demand is lifting all three major cloud providers — and that the infrastructure buildout continues to intensify.
Meta 🕐 1 min read

Meta Discloses $95 Billion AI Financing Plan

Meta revealed a financing plan of up to $95 billion for its AI data-center buildout, structured through forward stock sales and preferred equity rather than cash reserves. NVIDIA, AMD, and Broadcom expressed interest in up to $5 billion combined.

⚡ Why It Matters: The reliance on forward stock sales and preferred equity rather than cash signals that even cash-rich tech giants are treating AI infrastructure as large enough to warrant specialized financing approaches.
Industry 🕐 1 min read

CoreWeave Credit Spreads Remain Elevated Amid AI Infrastructure Boom

CoreWeave's credit-default-swap spreads stayed elevated this week — a market gauge of investor concern about default risk — even as they eased slightly from earlier-October highs.

⚡ Why It Matters: Elevated credit spreads for a major AI infrastructure lender suggest the market sees real risk that not all AI infrastructure investments will generate returns sufficient to service the debt funding them.
Governance 🕐 1 min read

Federal Reserve Holds Rates Steady Amid Big Tech Earnings Week

The U.S. Federal Reserve held interest rates steady on October 28 in a 10–2 vote, with markets debating whether a December move is likely. The decision landed in the middle of the week's Big Tech earnings run.

⚡ Why It Matters: For organizations tracking AI industry dynamics, macro context matters: the cost of capital influences how aggressively companies can finance the massive infrastructure buildouts currently underway.
Research 🕐 1 min read

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro Available via API, Open Weights Coming November 4

China's Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro via an OpenAI-compatible API with a 1-million-token context window. Full open-weight files are scheduled for November 4, just after this report's window closes.

⚡ Why It Matters: The upcoming open-weight release will let organizations run the model on their own infrastructure, an important option for those with data sovereignty or customization requirements.

🔭 What to Watch Next Week

Watch for Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro open-weight release on November 4, Anthropic's IPO roadshow and pricing, and whether independent mathematicians and physicists confirm or dispute OpenAI's claimed scientific breakthroughs. Microsoft's actual buyout participation numbers will reveal how large this restructuring turns out to be in practice.
